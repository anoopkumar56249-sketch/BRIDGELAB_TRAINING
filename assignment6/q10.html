Walking the long way
home.

Words by Noor Shah12 September 20259 min read

IN THIS PIECE

The beginningThe middle milesWhat remains

The road was not waiting for us. It did not know our names, our destination, or the careful time we had set aside to reach it.

We left after the last meeting, carrying two cups of coffee and one paper map between us. By the time the city thinned into fields, the map had become less useful than the changing light.

There is a freedom in choosing the longer route. Small landmarks become companions: the red gate, the bent tree, the house with blue shutters. We stopped often, not because we were tired, but because stopping made the distance visible.

"A journey changes shape when you stop measuring it only by arrival."

At dusk, we found the road again. Home was still ahead, but the walk had already given us something better than a shortcut: a way of paying attention.
